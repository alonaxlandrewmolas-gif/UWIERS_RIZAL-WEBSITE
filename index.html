    ABOUT DR. JOSE RIZAL

    ______________________________________________________

    Jose Protacio Rizal Mercado y Alonso Realonda was born on June 19, 1861, in Calamba, Laguna. His parents were Francisco Mercado and Teodora Alonso, and he was the seventh of their eleven children.


    He studied at the Ateneo Municipal de Manila and the University of Santo Tomas, then continued his medical studies in Madrid Spain, Paris, and Heidelberg. He became an ophthalmologist, and he was also a writer, poet, painter, sculptor, and reformer who is said to have spoken more than 20 languages.

    IMPORTANT FACTS

    ______________________________________________________

  • He founded La Liga Filipina in 1892, a group that pushed for peaceful reform.
  • He was exiled to Dapitan, Zamboanga, from 1892 to 1896, where he taught, farmed, and treated patients.
  • He was executed by firing squad on December 30, 1896, at Bagumbayan
    • (now Luneta Park), Manila.
